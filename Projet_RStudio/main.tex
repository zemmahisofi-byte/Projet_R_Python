\hypertarget{objective}{%
\section{Objective}\label{objective}}

The objective of this R analysis is to explore the reproducibility of
smartphone-based accelerometry for quantifying cerebellar tremor
frequency across repeated grasping tasks and across three consecutive
days.

The analysis is based on the dominant tremor frequency and RMS amplitude
extracted from Python using FFT.
